Story Splitter

Share your story on Discord

Paste or upload your story. It gets cut into Discord-sized code blocks at paragraph and sentence breaks, so you can copy and post them one at a time.

.txt, .md or .docx

Bold, italic, underline and strikethrough from Word, Google Docs or web pages are converted to Discord marks (**bold**, *italic*, __underline__, ~~strike~~) when you paste or upload. The marks show as-is inside the code blocks, ready to copy, and count toward the character limit.
